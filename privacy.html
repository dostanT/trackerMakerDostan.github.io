Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-18

Introduction

Tracker Maker is an iOS app that helps users create and customize personal trackers for habits, goals, and routines. Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your options regarding that data.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous device data: such as iOS version and device model, used to ensure app compatibility.
  • Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA): requested via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt to display personalized ads through Google AdMob.
  • Anonymous analytics data: events like feature usage and crash reports, used to improve performance and user experience.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or precise location directly within the app.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party platforms to support the app’s functionality:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying ads and measuring ad performance. View their Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — for managing in-app purchases and subscriptions. View their Privacy Policy.

How We Use the Data

  • To serve relevant ads and measure advertising effectiveness.
  • To support in-app purchases, subscriptions, and restoration of paid content.
  • To improve performance, usability, and overall experience of Tracker Maker.

Data Sharing & Security

  • We do not sell or rent any personal data to third parties.
  • Access to analytics and ad data is limited to the developer and integrated services listed above.
  • We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data integrity and confidentiality.

User Choices

You can control data collection and ad personalization through iOS settings:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and toggle permissions for Tracker Maker.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes stated above.
  • If you wish to request deletion of data associated with your device, contact us at the email below.

Children’s Privacy

Tracker Maker is not designed for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has shared information with us, contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date will reflect the changes.
