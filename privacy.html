Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-18

Introduction

Tracker Maker is an iOS app that helps users create and track personal habits, goals, and routines. Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your options regarding that data.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to provide our service:

  • Local app data: Your habits, progress, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults and App Groups. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • In-app purchase data: When you make purchases, RevenueCat (our payment processor) handles the transaction. We do not have access to your payment information.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, or any other personal data.

Third-Party Services

We use one trusted third-party platform:

  • RevenueCat — for managing in-app purchases and subscriptions only. View their Privacy Policy.

How We Use the Data

Your habit data and progress are stored locally on your device and used solely to provide the app's core functionality. In-app purchase data is processed by RevenueCat to enable premium features.

Data Sharing & Security

  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal data with third parties.
  • All your habit data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

User Choices

  • Delete the app to remove all local data.
  • Manage in-app purchases through your Apple ID settings.
  • Contact us to request assistance with data management.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your habit data is stored locally on your device only. When you delete the app, all data is automatically removed. In-app purchase data is managed by Apple and RevenueCat according to their respective policies.

Children's Privacy

Tracker Maker is not designed for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children as we do not collect any personal data.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will reflect the changes.
